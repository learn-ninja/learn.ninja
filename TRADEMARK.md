# Trademark and brand guidelines

The code in this repository is open source under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE), and the example lessons and docs are [CC-BY-4.0](LICENSE-CONTENT). Our brand is not. These guidelines explain what you can and can't do with it, so that people always know whether they're using learn.ninja or something built from it.

## What's covered

These are trademarks of Uxcel Inc, and neither license grants any rights to them (Apache-2.0 §6 says so for the code):

- **The name** "learn.ninja", and names that could be confused with it.
- **The logo**: `apps/web/app/icon.svg`, the favicon, and every version of it.
- **The files in [`brand/`](brand)**: the share images, icons, and banners.
- **The ninja**: the character as a whole, with his look, his name, his voice, and his lines.

The ninja's art (`packages/player/src/ninja.tsx`), lines (`packages/player-core/src/locales/voices/`), and voice clips (`apps/web/public/voice/`, `packages/player-core/src/voice-clips.json`) ship with the player under Apache-2.0, so contributors can improve him and a fork runs as it is. The license covers the files; the character stays our brand.

## What you can do

- Run, change, and host the code, as the license allows.
- Say your project is **"built with learn.ninja"**, **"a fork of learn.ninja"**, or **"compatible with the learn.ninja lesson format"**, as long as it's true.
- Write, teach, and talk about learn.ninja, and link to https://learn.ninja.
- Keep the ninja in the lesson player of your fork, doing what he does in ours.
- Use the logo to link to learn.ninja itself, for example in a list of tools or in an article about us.

## What needs our permission

- **Naming**: calling your product, fork, company, domain, package, or account "learn.ninja", or anything close enough to be confused with it.
- **Endorsement**: suggesting that Uxcel Inc or learn.ninja made, sponsors, endorses, or supports your project.
- **The logo as your branding**: using it, or something like it, as your product's logo, icon, or favicon.
- **The ninja as your mascot**: putting him (or a ninja drawn to look like him) in your marketing, ads, social accounts, merchandise, app-store listings, or anywhere outside the player.
- **Derivative ninjas**: presenting a modified ninja, his voice, or new lines as ours.
- **The files in `brand/`**, for anything other than linking to learn.ninja.

If you host a public fork, give it its own name and logo. The ninja may stay in the player.

## Asking

Want to do something that isn't listed here? Write to sensei@learn.ninja and tell us what you have in mind. We say yes more often than not.
